# 🧠 Debit–Credit Cheat Sheet (1 Page, Exam-Ready)

> **Goal:**  
> Tidak bingung lagi menentukan **Debit atau Credit** dalam < 10 detik.

---

## 🔑 Aturan Paling Dasar (HAFAL MATI)

- **Debit = Kiri**
- **Credit = Kanan**
- **Total Debit = Total Credit**

> ❗ Debit ≠ bad  
> ❗ Credit ≠ good  
> 👉 HANYA posisi

---

## ⚖️ Normal Balance (INTI SEMUA SOAL)

### 🔹 ADE vs LOR Rule

| Grup | Akun | Normal Balance |
|----|------|----------------|
| **ADE** | Assets | Debit |
|  | Drawings | Debit |
|  | Expenses | Debit |
| **LOR** | Liabilities | Credit |
|  | Owner’s Equity | Credit |
|  | Revenue | Credit |

📌 **Shortcut super cepat:**  
> **ADE = Debit**  
> **LOR = Credit**

---

## 📈📉 Aturan Naik–Turun (WAJIB PAHAM)

### Akun Normal Debit (ADE)

| Akun | Naik | Turun |
|----|----|----|
| Assets | Debit | Credit |
| Expenses | Debit | Credit |
| Drawings | Debit | Credit |

---

### Akun Normal Credit (LOR)

| Akun | Naik | Turun |
|----|----|----|
| Liabilities | Credit | Debit |
| Owner’s Equity | Credit | Debit |
| Revenue | Credit | Debit |

---

## 🧠 Logika di Balik Revenue & Expense

- Revenue **meningkatkan Owner’s Equity**  
  → ikut aturan **Equity (Credit)**

- Expense **menurunkan Owner’s Equity**  
  → kebalikannya (**Debit**)

---

## 🧾 Hubungan dengan Laporan Keuangan

### Balance Sheet Accounts
- Assets → Debit
- Liabilities → Credit
- Equity → Credit

### Income Statement Accounts
- Revenue → Credit
- Expense → Debit

---

## ⚡ Decision Tree (Pakai Saat Ujian)

**Langkah 1️⃣**  
Akun apa?  
→ Asset / Liability / Equity / Revenue / Expense / Drawings

**Langkah 2️⃣**  
Naik atau turun?

**Langkah 3️⃣**  
Pakai tabel ADE–LOR

> ✔ Done. Tidak perlu menghafal kasus per kasus.

---

## 📝 Contoh Kilat (Refleks Ujian)

### Terima cash dari customer
- Cash ↑ → Debit  
- Revenue ↑ → Credit  

---

### Bayar gaji
- Expense ↑ → Debit  
- Cash ↓ → Credit  

---

### Owner setor modal
- Cash ↑ → Debit  
- Owner’s Capital ↑ → Credit  

---

### Owner tarik uang pribadi
- Drawings ↑ → Debit  
- Cash ↓ → Credit  

---

### Beli equipment pakai utang
- Equipment ↑ → Debit  
- Accounts Payable ↑ → Credit  

---

## ⚠️ Exam Traps (Baca Sekali, Selamat Seumur Bab)

- Cash diterima ≠ selalu revenue  
- Debit ≠ selalu naik  
- Expense ≠ liability  
- Drawings ≠ expense  
- Revenue ≠ asset  

---

## 🧠 10-Second Mental Check

> **ADE naik → Debit**  
> **LOR naik → Credit**  
> **Turun? → kebalikannya**

Kalau ragu:
➡️ kembali ke **Owner’s Equity logic**
