## 1️⃣ What is Accounting? (LO 1)

### Definisi (Feynman)
**Accounting = proses untuk:**
1. **Identify** kejadian ekonomi  
2. **Record** kejadian tersebut  
3. **Communicate** hasilnya ke pengguna  

> *Accounting identifies, records, and communicates economic events.*

### Catatan penting
- Tidak semua aktivitas = transaksi
- Harus **mengubah posisi keuangan**

### Contoh
- Beli mesin → ✔ dicatat  
- Diskusi ide produk → ❌ tidak dicatat  

---

## 👥 Users of Accounting Information

### 🔹 Internal Users
- Owner
- Manager
- Employees  

📌 Fokus: *planning, controlling, decision making*

### 🔹 External Users
- Investors
- Creditors (bank)
- Government  

📌 Fokus: *profitability & solvency*

> ⚠️ **Exam trap:** Manager = internal user

## 2️⃣ Ethics, Principles, Assumptions (LO 2)

---

### 🧭 Ethics in Accounting

**Ethics = standar perilaku:**
- Right vs wrong
- Honest vs dishonest

📌 Tanpa etika → laporan keuangan tidak bisa dipercaya

#### Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX)
- Dibuat setelah skandal: Enron, WorldCom
- Tujuan: meningkatkan kepercayaan investor

---

### 📏 Accounting Principles (GAAP)

**GAAP = aturan pelaporan keuangan**

Standard setters:
- **FASB** (US)
- **IASB** (International / IFRS)

---

#### Measurement Principles

**1️⃣ Historical Cost Principle**
- Aset dicatat berdasarkan **harga beli**
- Lebih **reliable**

**2️⃣ Fair Value Principle**
- Aset/liabilitas dicatat pada **nilai pasar**
- Lebih **relevant**

📌 Trade-off: *relevance vs faithful representation*

---

### 📐 Accounting Assumptions

#### 1️⃣ Monetary Unit Assumption
- Hanya yang bisa diukur dengan uang yang dicatat

❌ Skill owner  
✔ Cash  

---

#### 2️⃣ Economic Entity Assumption ⭐
- **Uang bisnis ≠ uang pribadi**

📌 Owner bayar kebutuhan pribadi dari kas bisnis  
→ **Drawings**, bukan expense

> ⚠️ **Exam favorite**

---

## 3️⃣ Basic Accounting Equation (LO 3)

### Rumus utama
\[
Assets = Liabilities + Owner’s\ Equity
\]

### Interpretasi
- Assets = resources
- Liabilities = klaim kreditor
- Owner’s Equity = sisa hak pemilik

---

### Komponen

#### 🟢 Assets
- Cash
- Accounts Receivable
- Equipment  

➡️ Memberi manfaat di masa depan

#### 🔴 Liabilities
- Accounts Payable
- Notes Payable  

➡️ Kewajiban ke pihak lain

#### 🔵 Owner’s Equity
Naik karena:
- Investment by owner
- Revenue  

Turun karena:
- Expenses
- Drawings  

---

### Expanded Equation

$$Assets = Liabilities + Capital + Revenue - Expenses - Drawings$$


---

## 4️⃣ Transaction Analysis (LO 4)

### Transaksi
➡️ Kejadian ekonomi yang **mengubah**:
- Assets
- Liabilities
- Equity

📌 Setiap transaksi:
- Punya **dual effect**
- Persamaan harus tetap seimbang

---

### Contoh Singkat

**Owner invest cash**
- Cash ↑
- Capital ↑

**Pay expenses**
- Cash ↓
- Expenses ↑ → Equity ↓

**Earn revenue**
- Cash / A/R ↑
- Revenue ↑ → Equity ↑

> ⚠️ Terima cash ≠ selalu revenue  
> Bisa pelunasan piutang

---

## 5️⃣ Financial Statements (LO 5)

---

### 1️⃣ Income Statement
📌 **Period-based**

Menunjukkan:
- Revenue
- Expenses
- Net Income  

❌ Tidak termasuk:
- Investment
- Drawings  

---

### 2️⃣ Owner’s Equity Statement
📌 Menunjukkan perubahan modal selama periode

Butuh:
- Net income (dari income statement)

---

### 3️⃣ Balance Sheet ⭐
📌 **As of a specific date**

Menunjukkan:
- Assets
- Liabilities
- Owner’s Equity  

\[
Assets = Liabilities + Owner’s\ Equity
\]

---

### 4️⃣ Statement of Cash Flows
📌 Menjawab:
- Cash dari mana?
- Dipakai untuk apa?
- Sisa cash berapa?

---

### 🔗 Hubungan Antar Laporan
1. Income Statement → Net Income  
2. Net Income → Owner’s Equity Statement  
3. Ending Equity → Balance Sheet  
4. Balance Sheet + Income Statement → Cash Flow  

---

## 🧠 Exam Quick Summary

- Accounting = identify, record, communicate  
- Pisahkan uang pribadi & bisnis  
- Semua transaksi punya dual effect  
- Balance sheet = **specific date**  
- Income statement = **period**

---

## ⚠️ Common Exam Traps
- Manager ≠ external user  
- Drawings ≠ expense  
- Cash received ≠ always revenue  
- Assets dicatat historical cost (kecuali fair value cases)
