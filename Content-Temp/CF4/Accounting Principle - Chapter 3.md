# 📘 Chapter 3 — Adjusting the Accounts (Feynman Notes)

> **Big Picture:**  
> Chapter 3 menjawab pertanyaan:  
> **“Bagaimana memastikan laporan keuangan jujur secara WAKTU?”**  
>  
> Tanpa adjusting entries, laporan keuangan **bisa benar secara cash, tapi salah secara period**.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
1. Accrual basis & alasan adjusting entries  
2. Adjusting entries untuk **deferrals**  
3. Adjusting entries untuk **accruals**  
4. Adjusted trial balance  

---

## 1️⃣ Time Period & Accrual Basis (LO 1)

---

### ⏱ Time Period Assumption (Periodicity)

**Feynman version:**  
Bisnis berjalan terus, tapi akuntansi **dipotong-potong**.

Contoh:
- Januari
- Februari
- Maret

Tujuan:
> Menentukan *laba PER PERIODE*, bukan sejak bisnis berdiri.

---

### 📅 Fiscal Year vs Calendar Year
- **Calendar Year** → 1 Jan – 31 Des
- **Fiscal Year** → 12 bulan versi perusahaan

---

### 💰 Accrual Basis Accounting (WAJIB)

- Revenue dicatat **saat jasa dilakukan**
- Expense dicatat **saat beban terjadi**
- **Bukan saat cash diterima/dibayar**

📌 Sesuai **GAAP & IFRS**

---

### 💵 Cash Basis Accounting
- Revenue → saat cash diterima
- Expense → saat cash dibayar

❌ **Tidak sesuai GAAP / IFRS**

---

### 🎯 Dua Prinsip Utama

#### Revenue Recognition Principle
> Revenue diakui saat **performance obligation satisfied**

#### Expense Recognition Principle (Matching)
> Expenses follow revenues  
> Beban dicatat di periode yang sama dengan revenue terkait

---

## 2️⃣ Why Adjusting Entries? (LO 1)

---

### Masalah di Akhir Periode
- Ada revenue **sudah terjadi tapi belum dicatat**
- Ada expense **sudah terjadi tapi belum dibayar**
- Ada asset **sudah terpakai sebagian**

➡️ Trial balance **belum akurat**

---

### Adjusting Entries

**Adjusting entries = jurnal akhir periode** untuk:
- Menerapkan accrual accounting
- Memastikan akun **up-to-date**

📌 Ciri khas:
- Selalu melibatkan:
  - **1 Income Statement account**
  - **1 Balance Sheet account**
- Dibuat **setiap akhir periode**

---

## 3️⃣ Types of Adjusting Entries (Overview)

Adjusting Entries  
├── Deferrals (cash first)  
│ ├── Prepaid Expenses  
│ └── Unearned Revenues  
└── Accruals (record first)  
├── Accrued Revenues  
└── Accrued Expenses

---

## 4️⃣ Deferrals (LO 2)

> **Deferrals = cash terjadi dulu, pengakuan belakangan**

---

### A. Prepaid Expenses

#### Konsep (Feynman)
Bayar sekarang → pakai pelan-pelan.

Contoh:
- Insurance
- Rent
- Supplies
- Depreciation

---

#### Logika Akuntansi
- Awalnya → **Asset**
- Dipakai → jadi **Expense**

---

#### Pola Jurnal (HAFAL)
> **Expense ↑ (Debit)**  
> **Asset ↓ (Credit)**

---

#### Contoh: Supplies
- Supplies dibeli: 2,500  
- Sisa akhir bulan: 1,000  
- Dipakai: 1,500

Supplies Expense 1,500  
Supplies 1,500

---

#### Contoh: Prepaid Insurance
- Bayar 600 untuk 12 bulan
- Expense per bulan = 50

Insurance Expense 50  
Prepaid Insurance 50


---

### B. Depreciation (Prepaid Expense Khusus)

**Depreciation = alokasi biaya asset selama masa manfaat**

📌 BUKAN:
- Penilaian ulang
- Nilai pasar

---

#### Jurnal Depreciation
Depreciation Expense xxx  
Accumulated Depreciation xxx

📌 **Accumulated Depreciation**
- Contra asset
- Normal balance: **Credit**

---

### C. Unearned Revenues

#### Konsep (Feynman)
Terima uang dulu → kerja belakangan.

Contoh:
- Subscription
- Rent received in advance
- Customer deposits

---

#### Logika Akuntansi
- Awalnya → **Liability**
- Jasa dilakukan → jadi **Revenue**

---

#### Pola Jurnal (HAFAL)
> **Liability ↓ (Debit)**  
> **Revenue ↑ (Credit)**

---

#### Contoh
- Terima 1,200
- Jasa bulan ini = 400

Unearned Service Revenue 400  
Service Revenue 400

---

## 5️⃣ Accruals (LO 3)

> **Accruals = dicatat dulu, cash belakangan**

---

### A. Accrued Revenues

#### Konsep (Feynman)
Sudah kerja → belum ditagih / belum terima cash.

---

#### Pola Jurnal
> **Asset ↑ (Debit)**  
> **Revenue ↑ (Credit)**

---

#### Contoh
- Jasa 200 belum ditagih


Accounts Receivable 200  
Service Revenue 200
---

### B. Accrued Expenses

#### Konsep (Feynman)
Sudah pakai jasa / waktu orang lain → belum bayar.

Contoh:
- Salaries
- Interest
- Taxes

---

#### Pola Jurnal
> **Expense ↑ (Debit)**  
> **Liability ↑ (Credit)**

---

#### Contoh: Gaji
Salaries Expense 800  
Salaries Payable 800

---

#### Contoh: Bunga
- Loan: 30,000
- Rate: 10% per tahun → 250/bulan


Interest Expense 250
Interest Payable 250


---

## 6️⃣ Adjusted Trial Balance (LO 4)

---

### Apa itu Adjusted Trial Balance?

**Trial balance setelah semua adjusting entries diposting**

Tujuan:
- Membuktikan debit = credit
- **Dasar utama laporan keuangan**

---

### Urutan Lengkap

Unadjusted Trial Balance
→ Adjusting Entries
→ Adjusted Trial Balance
→ Financial Statements


---

### Ciri Penting
✔ Disusun setelah adjustment  
✔ Dipakai langsung untuk:
- Income Statement
- Owner’s Equity Statement
- Balance Sheet  

❌ Tidak menjamin bebas error

---

## 7️⃣ Hubungan ke Financial Statements

Semua laporan keuangan **dibuat dari Adjusted Trial Balance**:
- Income Statement
- Owner’s Equity Statement
- Balance Sheet

---

## ⚠️ Common Exam Traps

- Cash received ≠ revenue  
- Cash paid ≠ expense  
- Depreciation ≠ penurunan nilai pasar  
- Unearned revenue ≠ revenue  
- Accrued expense ≠ paid expense  

---

## 🧠 Chapter 3 — 1-Minute Summary

- Accrual accounting = record when earned/incurred
- Adjusting entries dibuat **akhir periode**
- Deferrals = cash dulu
- Accruals = dicatat dulu
- Prepaid expense → Asset → Expense
- Unearned revenue → Liability → Revenue
- Accrued revenue → AR + Revenue
- Accrued expense → Expense + Liability
- Adjusted TB = dasar laporan keuangan
